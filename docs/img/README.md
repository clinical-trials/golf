# Images

America (golfrange.org GRAA award profile). Used with the pro's involvement.

Coaching photos (Tom working with players) are published with the players'
permission, per Tom: `tom-harris-clinic.jpg` (and `tom-harris-clinic-2000.jpg`,
the same photo at 2000px, served to large screens via `srcset`) and
`tom-harris-group.jpg`. (`tom-harris-tech.jpg` was removed from the site.)

Photos of Tom on his own, supplied by the owner and in use on the page:
`tom-harris-lesson.jpg` (the hero — Tom mid-swing beside his bag; it replaced an
earlier coaching photo under the same filename) and `lesson-tee.jpg` (Tom on the
course with his bag, in the Player Development gallery).

Metadata: EXIF/XMP was stripped losslessly from the JPEGs here on 2026-11-18
(several had carried location data). Strip it from any new photo before
committing.
