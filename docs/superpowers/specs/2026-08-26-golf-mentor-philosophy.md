# The Golf Mentor Philosophy

**The mission in one sentence: convert curiosity into commitment — take someone
from "I'm interested in golf" to "I have a practice routine, people to play with,
and a reason to come back next week."**

The product's north star, supplied by the founder 2026-08-26. An ideal golf mentor
is not just a swing coach — they help a player build a durable relationship with
the game. Every feature should trace to one of these five traits.

## 1. Diagnose the person before the swing
Goals, confidence, schedule, budget, athletic background, fears. A junior, a
retired beginner, a competitive college player, and a new female golfer need very
different mentorship.
→ *In product:* Player Model + archetype layer; per-domain assessment; programs
per segment.

## 2. Make golf less intimidating
Etiquette, course behavior, practice habits, equipment, scoring, and expectations —
explained without making the player feel ignorant. Especially for beginners and
underrepresented golfers.
→ *In product:* etiquette-as-superpower weeks; quiet-tee-time suggestions; forward-
tees normalization; rentals-are-fine copy; women's and family programs.

## 3. Simplify improvement
One or two clear priorities, not ten swing thoughts. The player leaves knowing
exactly what to practice and why.
→ *In product:* prescription engine returns ONE fix + drills; honest allocation
("we're doing putting today"); one-feel-on-the-course teaching.

## 4. Care about retention, not just performance
Not only lower scores — enjoying golf enough to keep coming back. Wins early:
better contact, fewer lost balls, confidence, belonging.
→ *In product:* First 90 Days is a conversion program; benchmark retests make
progress visible; "book the next game before the parking lot."

## 5. Model the culture golf should have
Patient, honest, inclusive, reliable, connected. Introduce players to leagues,
partners, events, fittings, junior programs, women's groups, competitive pathways.
→ *In product:* play-with-strangers week; league/clinic roadmap; fitting referrals;
the no-fabrication, no-data-selling stance is the honesty trait made structural.
