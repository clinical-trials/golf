# 10 revenue ideas we haven't tackled yet (2026-09-07)

The site already monetises: 1-on-1 lessons, group classes, the $500/mo coaching
membership, corporate/team leagues, gift cards, referral credit, checkout
upsells, and (new) apparel/merch. These ten are **net-new** streams, ordered
roughly by leverage / ease.

1. **Self-paced digital courses (evergreen video).** Record Tom's method once —
   "Putting That Travels," "Your First Swing," "Own Your Wedges" — sell as a
   one-time or low monthly. Scales without Tom's time; the opposite of the live
   classes. Highest-margin product on the list.

2. **Freemium app subscription (~$12–19/mo).** A tier *below* the $500 coaching:
   the swing-capture + loop, course tool, progress dashboard and drills, minus
   live Tom. Turns the software itself into recurring revenue and a funnel to the
   premium tier.

3. **License the platform to other PGA pros ("Pocket Pro for Pros," B2B SaaS).**
   The original scale thesis: other coaches run their own branded academy on our
   rails for a monthly seat fee + rev-share. This is the venture-scale line.

4. **Affiliate pro-shop / gear.** "Tom's bag" and drill-specific training-aid
   picks with affiliate links (balls, rangefinders, wedges, mats). Commission
   revenue that also improves the student's kit; pairs with the Equipment class.

5. **Golf trips & retreats.** Curated 2–4 day experiences to the courses already
   featured (Pebble, Pinehurst, hill-country Texas) with Tom coaching on-site.
   High ticket, high margin, strong brand halo.

6. **Junior academy + summer / after-school camps.** A dedicated youth program
   (not just the family class) with seasonal enrollment, school partnerships and
   birthday/holiday camps — a recurring, high-volume local line.

7. **Corporate wellness benefit (employer-paid).** Sell golf as an HR wellness
   perk billed to the employer, distinct from team-building leagues — recurring
   B2B contracts, larger seat counts.

8. **Simulator bays / indoor off-season.** Partner-operate or rev-share sim bays
   for winter lessons, leagues and open practice — monetises the off-season and
   the "off-course golf" segment (ties to the PopStroke/Butler content).

9. **Paid tournaments & leagues (entry fees + skins).** Beyond corporate: public
   member-guests, junior tours, night-golf events — entry fees, sponsor packages
   and prize pools, powered by the live-scoring module already roadmapped.

10. **Tom Harris Golf Club — paid membership community.** A branded club (the
    badge!) with dues: priority booking, member events, a private feed/Q&A with
    Tom, gear discounts and merch drops. Recurring revenue + retention + a home
    for the apparel line.

**Honourable mentions:** golf-fitness/mobility subscription (TPI-style),
club-fitting with a fitter partner + commission, a coaching "lower your handicap
or your money back" premium guarantee tier, and a Tom Harris method book/ebook.

Every one of these is buildable on the existing stack (bookings, memberships,
Stripe, the module map) — no new fabrication required, just new offers.
