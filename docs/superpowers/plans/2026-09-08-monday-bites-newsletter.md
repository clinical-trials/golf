# Monday Bites — newsletter plan (2026-09-08)

**Format:** "Monday Bites" — one short, useful golf read every **Monday at noon**,
under the "The Club Sandwich" banner. Each issue is a single bite: a drill, a
course worth playing, a gear tip, or a rules/etiquette nibble. Delivered via
**Mailchimp** (embed/connect the signup on #newsletter when the account is live).
Tone: friendly, no-fluff, one idea you can act on this week. Lead magnet on
signup: Tom's one-page warm-up + the three-putt ladder drill.

## 10 opening concepts (one per Monday)

1. **The cheapest Pete Dye you can actually play.** Indiana's public Dye designs
   (Brickyard Crossing, The Fort) — championship pedigree at muni prices.
2. **The only four clubs a beginner needs.** Putter, wedge, 7-iron, a fairway
   wood — why this covers 90% of your first rounds (ties to the "one club" FAQ).
3. **Read any green in ten seconds.** The quick low-point method for pace + line.
4. **The five-minute range warm-up that saves three shots.** Tom's pre-round
   routine, in order.
5. **A course-ready starter outfit (women's).** Skort/polo/dress + where the
   dress code bends — ties to the women's what-to-wear builder.
6. **Three Austin munis where locals actually play.** Lions, Roy Kizer, Morris
   Williams — when to tee off and what they cost.
7. **Kill the three-putt on your carpet.** The ladder drill you can do indoors
   all week.
8. **Five things that make you welcome on any course.** Pace, ball marks, quiet,
   "fore," counting every stroke — etiquette without the stuffiness.
9. **One 7-iron, nine shots.** A practice game that builds feel and control fast.
10. **Off-season isn't off.** Keeping your game through winter with the sim and a
    short course (PopStroke, Butler) — plus a mobility bite.

## Backlog ideas (keep the pipeline full)
