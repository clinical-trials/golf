# Tom Harris — 20 Sticker Ideas (proposal, 2026-09-16)

**Concept.** Turn Tom's YouTube teaching into pocket-sized wisdom. Each sticker
starts as a real golf lesson Tom already teaches, then lands a second meaning
in everyday life — that double-take is the whole charm (and what makes people
buy *and* share them). Mix of **bumper stickers** (text) and **die-cut visual
stickers** (image). Print-on-demand, high margin, gift-ready, holiday-friendly.

## Production notes
- **Print-on-demand / drop-ship** (Printful, Sticker Mule, Gelato): no inventory.
- **Two formats:** 3"×1.5"–ish **bumper** (weatherproof vinyl) for the text lines;
  **die-cut** for the visual/brand ones.
- **Bundle for gifts/holidays:** a 5-pack "Clubhouse Wisdom" set; a $75 hat +
  matching sticker gift box; a free sticker in the first N course orders (cheap
  loyalty + brand spread).
- **Funnel tie-in:** each sticker's phrase links back to Tom's actual video —
  print a tiny "watch it" tag or QR so a sticker sells a lesson.
- **Test before printing:** the site's "Notify me" pro-shop capture + analytics
  tells us which lines people want before we commit to a run.

## Taste guardrails
Keep it clever, never crude — it's a teaching brand. Nothing that mocks the
student; the humor is with the golfer, not at them. Anything referencing a real
brand (e.g. G/FORE hats) stays factual/nominative, never implies endorsement.
