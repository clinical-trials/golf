The ambition

Every golf course on earth, the same way.

There are somewhere between 30,000 and 38,000 golf courses in the world, depending on who counts. This demo charts a handful to prove the method: hole geometry from OpenStreetMap, aerial photography from public imagery, strategy computed per player. The same pipeline runs on any course on request — nothing here is hand-drawn or invented.

Counts: Golfshake and World Golf Quest cite roughly 30,000; AllSquare catalogues around 33,000; the R&A's Golf Around the World survey reports 38,081 across 206 countries. We state the range rather than pick one. United States courses carry real 1-metre aerial photography today; elsewhere, high-resolution public imagery is thinner, so those courses show as vector routings until better sources are licensed.

Course geometry © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Aerial imagery from the USGS National Map, a United States government work in the public domain, requested live for the exact frame shown. Driver distances are Shot Scope published averages by handicap; the expected-strokes curve is fitted to Mark Broadie's published strokes-gained anchors. Nothing on this page is scraped from a commercial golf directory.
